Three videos that changed how I build. Each one below comes with the one thing I'd steal from it.

Watch

01 · The role

The best-paid marketing job of the next two years has no job description yet

Greg spends half an hour describing a hire almost no team has thought to make: someone who builds the systems marketing runs on — data, agents, evals — instead of running campaigns by hand. He puts the salary at $250K to $1M+ and then hands you a 30-day plan to become that person. This is the video I send when people ask what a marketing engineer actually does.

Greg Isenberg · Marketing Engineer: The $1M Job with AI Agents

02 · The work

They built a $5 ad that beats agency creative — live, on camera

Cody Schneider gives an agent the only three things it needs: data in a warehouse, write access to the ad platforms, and tools for images, video and leads. Then they ship static ads for Cursor and Late Checkout and a UGC avatar spot for about five dollars. The part worth stealing is the review layer — a swipe warehouse of references plus a creative-director agent that grades every ad before it ships.

Greg Isenberg & Cody Schneider · AI Agents are too good at marketing now

03 · The arbitrage

The next million-dollar business is a Facebook group nobody bothered to monetize

His wife needed a night nurse. The agency found one in a Facebook group. That is the whole thesis: agents can now mine groups, Marketplace and Instagram to match buyers with sellers in any city and any niche — and the clean list of names, needs, prices and trust signals becomes an asset you own. He demos it live for Montreal night nurses at $30 an hour, then maps the ladder from side hustle to vertical software.

Greg Isenberg · Meta Muse Will Create 1,000 Millionaires
